# My Journey to Engineering Leadership

Starting as a fresh graduate in 2015, I never imagined I'd be leading engineering teams at a major media company. The journey has been filled with challenges, learning opportunities, and countless moments of growth.

## The Beginning

When I first started my career, I was just another junior developer trying to prove myself. I spent countless hours learning, debugging, and building. Every code review was a learning opportunity, and every bug was a chance to understand systems better.

## Key Lessons Learned

Over the years, I've learned that becoming a leader is about more than technical skills:

- **Technical excellence is just the beginning** - While being a good programmer is important, it's only the foundation.
- **Communication skills are as important as coding skills** - Being able to explain complex concepts simply is invaluable.
- **Building trust with your team takes time but pays dividends** - Trust is earned through consistency and transparency.
- **Embrace failure as a learning opportunity** - Some of my biggest growth moments came from failures.

## Looking Forward

As I continue this journey, I'm excited about the opportunities ahead. Leading teams, building systems, and mentoring the next generation of engineers is incredibly fulfilling.
